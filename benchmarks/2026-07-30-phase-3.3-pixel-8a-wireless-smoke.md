## Result

All three tests passed in 43 seconds:

- both production sound banks decoded;
- dense standard metronome callbacks and frame audio completed;
- polyrhythm produced beat, rhythm, and coincident events.

The dense standard run recorded 48 events, callback p95 error of `1.079427` ms, 607 rendered chunks, and 2 `AudioTrack` underruns. Playback was audibly confirmed during the run. The underruns remain evidence for later performance qualification rather than being hidden by the passing functional smoke.

Device brightness, screen timeout, and speaker volume matched their pre-run values after completion.
