## Result

All three production audio tests passed in 30 seconds. Both sound banks decoded, dense standard playback produced monotonic callbacks and frame audio, and polyrhythm produced beat, rhythm, and coincident events.

A preliminary run exposed that the smoke stopped immediately after its final wall-clock callback, before the corresponding render block committed. It observed 47 rendered clicks, 600 rendered blocks, zero underruns, and otherwise matched frame accounting. The smoke now waits up to one second for the final scheduled render before teardown; production timing behavior was unchanged.

The wireless ADB transport was temporarily disconnected to satisfy the one-transport rule and automatically returned afterward. Device brightness drifted from `12` to `11` during execution and was restored explicitly.
