# Phase 3.5 Pixel 8a diagnostics and backend decision

- Date: 2026-07-30
- Source commit: `42a077e1827f3f03e222b8fa2abf9540df2dd2d4` plus the uncommitted Phase 3.5 working tree
- Device: Pixel 8a (`akita`)
- OS: Android 17
- Transport: one USB ADB transport
- Build: `benchmark`, debug-signed
- Route: built-in speaker
- Workload: 240 BPM, sixteenth subdivisions, five minutes

## Command

```bash
JAVA_HOME="/Applications/Android Studio.app/Contents/jbr/Contents/Home" \
ANDROID_HOME="$HOME/Library/Android/sdk" \
ANDROID_SERIAL="46131JEKB08004" \
./gradlew --no-daemon :app:connectedBenchmarkAndroidTest \
  -Pbeatclikr.testBuildType=benchmark \
  -Pandroid.testInstrumentationRunnerArguments.class=com.bfunkstudios.beatclikr.AudioEngineStressInstrumentedTest \
  -Pandroid.testInstrumentationRunnerArguments.stressDurationMinutes=5
```

## Result

The test passed in 300.157 seconds with 4,800 events and 59,976 rendered blocks. It reported zero scheduled drift, deadline misses, dropped events, route changes, and `AudioTrack` underruns. Intended, rendered, and written accounting matched at 14,394,240 frames; the final timestamp estimated 14,388,000 presented frames.

The obtained stream used `AudioTrack`, the built-in route, 48 kHz mono, a 240-frame burst, a 2,886-frame buffer, and low-latency performance mode. The burst period was 5 ms.

Mix duration upper bounds were 0.040 ms p50, 0.065 ms p95, and 0.225 ms p99; the exact maximum was 4.223145 ms. Blocking-write duration upper bounds were 0.625 ms p50 and 11.368657 ms at p95 and p99; the exact maximum was 101.851766 ms. Write timing measures device-drain pacing and is not used as mixer CPU cost.

## Decision

Retain the custom frame renderer on `AudioTrack`. Its mix p99 used at most 4.5% of the obtained burst period, and the run produced no recovery or underrun signal. AAudio or Oboe comparison remains a fallback only after a reproducible approved-gate failure survives `AudioTrack` tuning or required device coverage cannot be met.
